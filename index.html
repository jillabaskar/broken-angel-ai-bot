3D Ludo Game

Click "Roll Dice" to start!
Red Player 4 pieces in home
Blue Player 4 pieces in home
Green Player 4 pieces in home
Yellow Player 4 pieces in home
-

How to Play:

  • Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of home
  • Click on a piece to move it
  • Land on opponent's piece to capture it
  • Get all 4 pieces to the center to win!
  • Use mouse to rotate the board
